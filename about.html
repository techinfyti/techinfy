Our Story

Founded on a simple idea: IT should just work

TechInfy was founded in 2024 by a small group of systems engineers frustrated by slow, reactive IT support. We set out to build a company that treats software, data and support as one connected discipline — not a pile of disconnected vendors.

Today we support 4+ companies across healthcare, retail, logistics and finance, combining deep technical expertise with the responsiveness of an in-house team.
